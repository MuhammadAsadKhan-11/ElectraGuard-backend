Customer services manual

As a leading power supplier, MEPCO ensures customer satisfaction by addressing inquiries, resolving problems, and fostering high-quality relationships.
The manual outlines standardized strategies, excellent practices, and key concepts to enhance consumer interactions, ensuring consistency and performance in service delivery. Tariff Structure
Empowering Your Energy Choices
MEPCO’s tariff is designed to ensure fair and transparent pricing for electricity consumption across its service areas. It consists of various categories tailored to residential, commercial, and industrial customers, with distinct rates based on consumption levels.
Additionally, MEPCO offers low price lists for low-income households, promoting affordability and accessibility to electricity. By maintaining a balanced and competitive tariff structure, MEPCO aims to encourage accountable electricity intake and help sustainable regional development.

MEPCO Mobile App
Convenient Energy Management at Your Fingertips
Use the MEPCO mobile app and stay connected and informed with real-time notifications, making your energy management experience seamless and efficient.
Bill Estimator
The Bill Estimator function allows customers to calculate their upcoming electricity payments based on real-time utilization facts.
Power Complaint
Customers can easily complain about power interruptions through the app, which ensures prompt responses and efficient trouble decisions.
Load Shedding Schedule
You can stay informed with actual-time notifications regarding load-shedding schedules, service outages, and essential MEPCO announcements.
Smart Monitoring
Smart monitoring allows customers to analyze their power energy usage patterns, promotes power conservation, and informs energy usage selection.
Real-Time Consumption
The app contains real-time consumption data, allowing customers to track their electricity bills and control their consumption effectively.
