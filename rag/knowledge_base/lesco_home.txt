The Organization
LESCO, Lahore Electric Supply Company, is an electricity
distribution company. It serves multiple
districts including Lahore, Okara, Sheikhupura, Nankana,
and Kasur. LESCO emerged in 2001 from the Lahore Area
Electricity Board. It operates with numerous substations and
ensures reliable power supply and customer services.
